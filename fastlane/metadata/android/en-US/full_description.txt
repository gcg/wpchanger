Turn your own photos into a wallpaper that keeps changing.

Pick a few favorite shots or a whole album, choose how often you want something new, and Wallpaper Changer takes care of the rest, quietly, in the background.

<b>How it works</b>
• Add photos with the system photo picker, or import an entire folder at once
• Pick an interval: every 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, 6 hours, or once a day
• Organize photos into stacks like Landscapes or Friends, and switch which one rotates
• Choose where it goes: home screen, lock screen, or both
• Tap "Change Wallpaper Now" whenever you want a fresh look

<b>Smart shuffle</b>
Every photo in your pool gets its turn before any of them repeats, and you'll never see the same photo twice in a row.

<b>Easy on your battery</b>
Rotation uses Android's own job scheduler, pauses automatically in Battery Saver or when the battery is low, and picks up again on its own. Nothing runs in between changes.

<b>Private by design</b>
• No internet permission: your photos never leave your phone
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Photos are copied into the app's private storage, so rotation keeps working even if the originals move

<b>Thoughtful details</b>
• Duplicate photos are skipped automatically
• Correct orientation for photos taken in portrait or landscape
• Optional notification with a preview each time the wallpaper changes
• Material You design that follows your wallpaper's colors, in light and dark mode
• Keeps rotating after a restart

Wallpaper Changer is open source: github.com/gcg/wpchanger
