# Privacy Policy — Wallpaper Changer

_Last updated: October 3, 2026_

Wallpaper Changer ("the app") is developed by Guney Can Gokoglu. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: **nothing leaves your device.**

## Data the app accesses

- **Photos you choose.** When you add photos or a folder, the app copies those images into its own private storage on your device so it can set them as your wallpaper later. It only sees the photos you explicitly pick.
- **App settings.** Your chosen interval, target screen, and similar preferences are stored on your device.

## Data the app collects or shares

None. The app:

- does not request internet access and cannot send data anywhere
- has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting, or third-party tracking SDKs
- does not sell or share any data with anyone

## Backups

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's **settings** in your device backup. Imported photos are explicitly excluded from backups. Backups are handled by Android under your Google account, not by the developer.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| :--- | :--- |
| Set wallpaper | To change your home and lock screen wallpaper |
| Run at startup | To resume scheduled rotation after your phone restarts |
| Notifications (optional) | To tell you when the wallpaper changes, only if you turn it on |

## Deleting your data

Remove photos in the app (individually or with **Clear All**), or uninstall the app to delete everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
